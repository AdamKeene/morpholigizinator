Electric vehicles are becoming increasingly popular due to their environmental benefits and technological advancements. The core component of an EV is its battery pack, typically made of lithium-ion cells. Battery technology is rapidly evolving, focusing on increasing energy density, reducing charging times, and improving safety. Research into solid-state batteries promises even greater range and faster charging. The supply chain for battery materials like cobalt and nickel is a critical consideration for sustainable production. Governments worldwide are implementing policies to promote EV adoption and support the development of charging infrastructure.
